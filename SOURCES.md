### RenaiSun reference deck

A RenaiSun competition PDF supplied for comparison was reviewed for **story structure and pitch design**.  
The full third-party PDF is **not redistributed in this repository**. This avoids republishing another team's complete document without a clear license. The report contains the derived analytical comparison only.

## Source-use rule

Third-party results are **benchmarks**, not Cultivator performance claims.  
For example, HydroNeo's reported energy savings are used to test whether Cultivator pricing could fit within plausible customer value; the report does **not** claim Cultivator has already achieved HydroNeo's results.
