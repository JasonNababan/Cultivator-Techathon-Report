## Pilot success criteria

The closed-loop system should not scale until field data answers:

1. **Safety** — Does DO remain within the defined safety envelope?
2. **Energy** — Does kWh/pond-day decrease versus comparable baseline operation?
3. **Current sensing** — Can the system reliably detect relevant abnormal states?
4. **Trust** — Are false alerts, uptime, and offline behavior acceptable to farmers?

The strongest commercial proof will be:

> **Energy falls without weakening the DO safety margin.**
